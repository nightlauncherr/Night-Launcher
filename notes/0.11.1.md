Maintenance update: cape fix and fixes found in a full review.

- **Capes:** an equipped Kestrel cape now shows on your own player even if your Minecraft account has an official cape (most accounts do). Choose No cape to show the official one again. Other players still see your official cape
- The in-game cape menu now warns when capes are switched off in Options > Skin Customization, which hides every cape
- The update window shows release notes as plain text, without Markdown symbols
- Custom capes on the launcher's Capes page: the status text no longer overlaps the Remove button
- Your settings, accounts and game files are kept
