Open Map PvP is here.

- **OPEN MAP PVP button:** a new button under Sumo Practice starts the game straight into the Open Map lobby. You can also open it from Singleplayer with **CREATE OPEN MAP WORLD**
- **Open map:** a 256 × 256 grassland arena with gentle hills and scattered trees, made the first time you open it and kept as you left it after that
- **Diamond kit:** diamond armor, an iron sword, 64 oak planks, a fishing rod, 32 snowballs and 3 golden apples, refilled at the start of every round and kept in the hotbar slots you put them in
- **Night Bot:** an AI opponent with the same kit (no golden apples) that strafes, combos, uses the rod and snowballs, dodges your throws and builds: pillars for the high ground, walls for cover, stairs up to you and bridges across gaps. It learns your habits and adapts its difficulty to your results, or you can pick Easy, Medium, Hard or Expert, and a playstyle
- **Rounds:** press **START** and rounds keep going on their own; the first death ends the round and blocks placed during it are cleared. `/stopopenmap` ends the match and takes you back to the lobby
- **Open Map menu:** press **M** any time for your stats (wins, losses, streaks, the bot's rod and snowball hits), the opponent settings and the controls
