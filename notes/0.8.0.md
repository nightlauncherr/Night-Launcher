The visual overhaul: a new sword animation, 20 new 3D kill effects and new combat and world visuals.

- **Kestrel slash (new sword animation):** the blade turns to face you and cocks back, sweeps up and over to the left with a glowing crescent trail in your sword's own colour, follows through below the screen and rises smoothly back into place. It runs on real time, so it looks the same at any FPS, and clicking again mid-swing blends instead of snapping
- It is visual only: it starts when Minecraft's own swing starts and never delays attacks or changes cooldowns, timing, reach, hit registration or your rotation. Blocking and using items work as before
- **Animations tab (Right Shift):** sword style (Kestrel slash, Smooth, Chop or Vanilla), slash trail on/off and trail colour, separate style for axes, pickaxes, shovels, hoes and fishing rods, speed and intensity, smooth item switching, smooth blocking, an optional visual swing while blocking, idle sway, a preview button and "Restore vanilla animations"
- **20 new 3D kill effects** in a new 3D category, drawn with real geometry: Lightning strike, Energy explosion, Black hole, Dragon burst, Crystal explosion, Fire tornado, Frost spikes, Portal rift, Meteor impact, Soul ascension, Electric sphere, Dark energy, Rainbow explosion, Star rain, Void explosion, Magic circle, Flame wings, Shattered dimension, Neon pulse and the Kestrel talon signature. They still play only for players you kill yourself, once per kill
- **Visuals tab:** hit sparks on your own hits (gold on critical hits), trails behind arrows, pearls, snowballs, eggs and potions, arrow impact bursts, experience orb glow and optional refined rain and snow, each with its own switch, plus "Restore vanilla visuals" and a button that restores Minecraft's look everywhere
- The launcher's Cosmetics page previews all the new effects
- Your settings, accounts, game files, mods, resource packs, capes, skies and kill effects are kept
