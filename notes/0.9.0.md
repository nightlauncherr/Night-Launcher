A brand-new Kestrel main menu, with your own character at the centre.

- **New main menu:** Minecraft's menu is replaced everywhere (start-up, leaving a world, disconnecting) by Kestrel's own: a dark backdrop with flowing ribbons, the Kestrel logo, and a clean text menu for Singleplayer, Multiplayer, Realms, Mods, Minecraft Settings, Kestrel Settings and Quit Game
- **Your character runs in:** your skin, as a 3D player with every second-layer detail and classic or slim arms, runs in from the side, slows to a stop, turns to face you and stands with your name above it. It breathes slightly and glances towards your mouse
- **SKIN button** under the character opens the redesigned skin screen: your skin large on the right (drag to turn it), and on the left import a PNG, load a NameMC profile link, pick from your library, switch arms, apply, or restore your account skin. The menu character changes as soon as you apply a skin
- The first time you open the game each session there is a short "click to enter" intro
- Skins are still local: other players see your account skin
- Your settings, accounts, game files, mods, resource packs, capes, skies and kill effects are kept
