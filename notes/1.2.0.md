Redeem codes for Night cosmetics.

- **Redeem page:** enter a code to unlock Night cosmetics for the Minecraft account you're playing on. Each account redeems a code once, and its rewards stay with it on every instance and PC
- **Rewards stay with their account:** switching accounts, in the launcher or in game, shows only that account's cosmetics. A cosmetic equipped by another account comes back when you switch back to it
- **Cleaner cosmetics menus:** the launcher and in-game cape lists only show what the current account can use
