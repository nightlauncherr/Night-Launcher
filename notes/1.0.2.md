Capes work again, and Night players can now see each other's capes.

- **Fixed: equipped capes not showing.** Cape mods such as WaveyCapes blocked Night's cape completely, in game and in the menus. Night now draws capes with its own cape model, so your equipped cape shows whatever other mods you use, and whether or not your account owns an official cape
- **Cape sharing:** other Night players see the built-in or Mojang cape you equip, and you see theirs. Your account is verified through Mojang's session server, the same check servers use; your login is never sent to Night. Uploaded capes stay on your PC. Turn it off with "Share my cape with Night players"
- Night's own internet requests work on Minecraft's old Java 8
