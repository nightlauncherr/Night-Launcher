A smoother main menu, and the character now looks where your mouse is.

- **Main menu runs at your frame rate:** Minecraft 1.8.9 limits every menu screen to 30 FPS when you are not in a world, which made the new menu's animations choppy. Kestrel's main menu and Skin screen now use your own Max Framerate setting (and VSync, if it is on), the same as in game. On a test PC it went from 30 FPS to 120 FPS with Max Framerate 120 and VSync on (about 2,300 FPS with Unlimited and VSync off), and the slowest frames dropped from about 35 ms to about 10 ms
- **Head tracking fixed:** the character's head used to turn the wrong way. It now turns left, right, up and down towards the pointer, smoothly, within natural limits, and goes back to facing forward when the pointer leaves the window. It works the same at every window size and GUI scale
- The menu's background draws with far fewer draw calls; it looks exactly as before
- Nothing else in the menu changed: layout, entrance, idle, SKIN button and skin screen are as they were
- Your settings, accounts, game files, mods, resource packs, capes, skies, skins and kill effects are kept
