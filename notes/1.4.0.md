Sumo practice is here.

- **SUMO PRACTICE button:** a new button under Play starts the game straight into the Sumo lobby. You can also open it from Singleplayer with **CREATE SUMO WORLD**
- **Official Sumo map:** a hand-built arena and lobby, installed the first time you open Sumo and kept as you left it after that
- **Night Bot:** an AI opponent that strafes, W-taps, jumps and pushes you toward the edge. It learns your habits and adapts its difficulty to your results, or you can pick Easy, Medium, Hard or Expert, and a Balanced, Aggressive, Defensive or Strafer playstyle
- **Rounds:** press **START SUMO** and rounds keep going on their own, with the result shown on screen. `/stopsumo` ends the match and takes you back to the lobby
- **Sumo menu:** press **M** any time for your stats (wins, losses, streaks, fastest win), the opponent settings and the controls
