Nine new PvP and visual QoL features in game, all under Right Shift → **QoL** and all off until you switch them on.

- **Hit Color:** players you hit flash a colour you choose once the server confirms the hit, then fade back. You can set the duration, intensity and fade
- **Hit Delay Fix:** removes vanilla's half-second click lock after missing, and restarts the swing animation on every click. It changes responsiveness only: no extra attacks, reach or packets
- **Low Health Alert:** a banner and optional sound when the player you're fighting drops to your threshold. It says so when a server hides health
- **Opponent Health Prediction:** your target's health, exact when the server sends it, otherwise "~" estimated from your confirmed hits (armour, Protection, Sharpness, crits, blocking, absorption)
- **Block Counter:** "BLOCKS: 112" for the placeable blocks in your hotbar, with icon, held stack and block-type filter. Move it in the HUD editor
- **Bridge Assist:** a placement preview, a face highlight and an edge guide that lights up when you're in position. It is visual only
- **Clear Water:** clearer, brighter underwater view with a fainter overlay
- **No Blindness:** removes blindness's dark fog. The effect itself stays, and some servers (e.g. Hypixel) don't allow this
- **Weather Customizer:** rain, snow, splashes and rain sounds on or off, intensity, and a clear-weather mode. These are local only
- Your settings, accounts and game files are kept
