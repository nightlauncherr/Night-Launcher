A brand-new settings menu and a remade sky collection.

- **New settings menu:** Right Shift now opens a redesigned menu with categories in a sidebar (Combat, Movement, Visuals, HUD, World, Cosmetics, Performance and Other), settings grouped into cards with a short description under each one, smooth animations and scrolling. Right Shift or Esc closes it
- **Search:** start typing anywhere in the menu to find a setting by name, or a cape, sky or kill effect. Click a result to jump straight to it
- **Better controls:** animated switches, sliders that show their value, dropdown lists, a colour picker with a palette and hex input, and your Night keybinds can now be changed right inside the menu (under Other)
- **New skies:** 35 new skies in sharper detail across Space, Sunsets, Clouds & Aurora, Dark, Fantasy, Abstract, Simple and Night. If you had a sky that was removed, you get the closest new one instead
- **Smoother skies:** sky edges blend without visible seams, and skies load without a hitch and use less memory
- **Kill effects:** more server kill messages are recognised, and in Sumo and Open Map your effect only plays when you actually hit the bot before it died
- **Main menu:** the code splash text is gone
